# Vision

## What is AI Native Gov?

AI Native Gov is a **надгосударственный (supranational) global AI layer** that models institutional topology — checks and balances across deliberative, executive, judicial, and enforcement layers — as a coherent system for understanding and forecasting geopolitical and policy dynamics.

It is not a government. It is a **modeling framework** where AI institutions mirror real-world separation of powers, enabling:

- Structured deliberation on signals (Parliament)
- Policy execution simulation (Executive)
- Dispute resolution and precedent (Judiciary)
- Cross-border coordination (Interpol)
- Domain expertise (Ministries)

## Why it exists

Geopolitical signal streams are noisy, fragmented, and often interpreted without institutional context. AI Native Gov provides:

1. **Topology** — explicit maps of how layers intersect and constrain each other
2. **Checks & balances** — no single layer dominates interpretation
3. **Downstream feeds** — validated signals flow to Errorlogy (errors/forecasts) and politic.bar (politifi streams)
4. **Reproducible scenarios** — cascade examples (e.g. Trump–Macron) as test cases

## Core principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Supranational | Models operate above single-state framing; multi-actor, multi-jurisdiction |
| Institutional | Signals pass through modeled institutions, not raw aggregation |
| Checks & balances | Parliament, Executive, Judiciary, Interpol cross-check each other |
| Feed, don't replace | Errorlogy and politic.bar consume outputs; umbrella defines contracts |
| Agent-native | Designed for Cursor/agent workflows from a single umbrella workspace |

## Success criteria

- Agents can reason about geopolitical cascades using institutional topology
- Integration contracts are stable enough for child repo implementation
- Scenario docs (e.g. trump-macron-cascade) demonstrate end-to-end flow
- Child repos implement without duplicating umbrella strategy

## Long-term direction

- Live signal ingestion with institutional routing
- Schema-validated cross-repo pipelines
- Ministry expansion (defense, finance, climate, tech)
- Public politifi surfaces on politic.bar fed by validated umbrella outputs
