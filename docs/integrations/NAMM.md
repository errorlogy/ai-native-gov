## Role of NAMM in the ecosystem

NAMM tests whether **machine-native artifacts** can certify mathematical structure before compact human projection exists.

AI Native Gov produces **topology hypotheses** that may become formalizable:

- Graph invariants on α-propagation
- Aggregation rules for politifi multi-card components
- Holdout tests on FPD forecast envelopes
- Attractor thresholds (AT1–AT3) as predicates

NAMM is **optional verification** — not narrative authority.
