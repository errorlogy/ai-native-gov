# Philosophy — Epistemic Frame

**Epistemic label:** `INSTITUTIONAL_MODEL` — lore and heuristic framing for the AI Native Gov simulator. Not a scientific verdict, taxonomic claim, or statement of real-world authority.

---

## Homo loquens / pre-sapiens heuristic

*Homo sapiens* is less a taxonomic description than a civilizational compliment. Statistically, the species is defined not by “sapiens,” but by speech, imitation, signaling, and narrative coordination.

In that sense, **Homo loquens** — or perhaps even **Homo pre-sapiens** — may be the more accurate heuristic designation for an **unfinished evolutionary phase**: a species that coordinates through story and signal faster than it can reliably minimize governance error.

This framing is a modeling lens, not biology. It motivates why institutional reasoning layers and error measurement matter.

---

## Governance error and cognitive extension

The modeling hypothesis of this umbrella:

> Homo cannot carry out governance / management with minimized errors without cognitive extension along an **AI → AGI → ASI** progression.

Within AI Native Gov:

| Idea | Meaning in the simulator |
|------|--------------------------|
| AI → AGI → ASI | Spectrum of institutional reasoning capacity — layers that extend coordination, not a mythology of replacement |
| Error minimization | Research and measurement problem, not a claim that ASI “solves” politics |
| Unfinished phase | Narrative coordination alone under-constrains institutional outcomes; topology + checks & balances + measurement are required |

**Not claimed:** sovereign AI government, legitimate rule, or that machines displace human moral authority.

**Claimed (as model):** governance simulation that aims at lower coordination error needs structured institutional layers plus an engine that can measure error signals.

---

## Human oversight remains structurally required

Even at high autonomy dial settings, humans remain the hard-stop, appeal, and audit surface. See [AI_HUMAN_OVERSIGHT.md](institutions/AI_HUMAN_OVERSIGHT.md):

- `human_override_always` — cannot be disabled by AI agents
- veto, appeal, audit, dial rollback
- outputs from the panel are `HUMAN_DETERMINATION`, not computational verdicts

Cognitive extension does not erase oversight. It increases the need for a non-bypassable human layer.

## Autonomy dials and the unfinished phase

In the AI Native Gov simulator, **autonomy dials** encode the spectrum of *homo* vs AI participation during the unfinished phase:

| Dial region | Heuristic reading |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Low AI / Homo-primary | Narrative coordination dominant; AI advises |
| Mid / Co-governance | Shared execution; human veto retained |
| High AI / AI-primary + oversight | Institutional roles filled by AI layers; Human Oversight Panel structurally required |

World-scale heterogeneous dials: [GLOBAL_AI_GOVERNANCE.md](institutions/GLOBAL_AI_GOVERNANCE.md). Single-stack dials: [AI_GOVERNMENT_OVERVIEW.md](institutions/AI_GOVERNMENT_OVERVIEW.md).

Dials are scenario parameters for modeling — not deployment recommendations and not proof of readiness.
